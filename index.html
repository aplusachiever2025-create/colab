06 / A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDERS

Curiosity is a serious advantage.

INK & QUILL is a Singapore-based IB humanities tuition centre founded by three medical students. We believe that the habits behind strong humanities work—careful observation, intellectual curiosity and precise communication—matter far beyond the classroom.

We teach students not just to find an answer, but to understand why it works.
